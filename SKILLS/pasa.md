# PASA

**Keeping Parents in Sync with Everything Regarding Their Child's Education**

Founder: Khanya Mgebisa

---

## Executive Summary

PASA is a parent-school engagement platform built to solve a simple but significant problem:

> Parents often have limited visibility into what is happening in their children's educational lives.

Schools communicate important information every day. Parents are expected to support learners, attend meetings, monitor academic progress and respond to disciplinary matters. Yet much of this information is communicated through fragmented channels that are unreliable, difficult to track and often dependent on learners themselves.

PASA creates a direct connection between schools and verified parents, ensuring that important educational information is accessible, visible and accountable.

The platform enables schools to communicate academic activities, examinations, tests, extra classes, events, meetings and learner-related updates through a secure and structured system.

PASA has already been developed into a working Minimum Viable Product and is preparing for pilot adoption.

---

## The Challenge

Every day, schools generate information that parents need to know. However, communication often relies on:

- Printed letters
- Learner diaries
- WhatsApp groups
- SMS messages
- Verbal communication through learners

The result is predictable. Parents miss information. Schools repeatedly communicate the same information. Learners sometimes exploit information gaps. Accountability becomes difficult.

The challenge is not a lack of communication. The challenge is **visibility**.

---

## Why PASA Was Created

PASA was inspired by real situations observed in schools and communities.

One incident involved a learner being suspended from school and instructed to return with a parent. Instead of informing the actual parent, the learner convinced another adult to pretend to be their guardian. The school believed the matter had been resolved. The real parent never learned what happened.

In another case, learners regularly told parents they were attending extra classes after school. Parents had no reliable way to verify this information. Some learners attended legitimate academic activities. Others used the explanation to spend time elsewhere.

The same challenge appears when learners remain at school after normal hours. Parents are often unaware of schedule changes, academic activities or transport implications.

These situations reveal a common problem. Parents are expected to support their children, yet they often lack visibility into important events affecting their education.

PASA was created to bridge this gap.

## The Vision

Parents cannot support what they cannot see.

PASA exists to create a future where every parent has visibility into the educational journey of their child. A future where schools communicate more effectively. A future where learners benefit from stronger collaboration between home and school.

## The Solution

PASA provides a structured parent-school engagement platform. Schools publish important information directly through PASA. Verified parents gain visibility into information relevant to their children.

The platform serves as a trusted source of educational information and activity. Rather than relying on fragmented communication channels, schools and parents interact through a single system designed specifically for educational engagement.

---

## How PASA Works

Schools are organised according to the existing education structure:

**Province → District → School**

Each school can have:
- Principals
- School Administrators
- Teachers

Role-based access ensures that users only access information relevant to their responsibilities.

Parents create accounts independently. To access learner information, a parent submits a learner-link request. The request includes:

- Province
- District
- School
- Learner Name
- Learner Number
- Relationship to Learner

The school verifies and approves the request before access is granted. This ensures that schools remain the source of truth while maintaining learner privacy and security.

- Tests
- School events

## Business Model

PASA is designed to maximise accessibility for schools. Schools receive access to core functionality at no cost. Parents can access a free account linked to a single learner profile.

Future premium subscriptions may provide:
- Multiple learner profiles
- Advanced notifications
- Cross-school scheduling features
- AI-powered educational assistance

The objective is to maintain broad accessibility while supporting sustainable growth.
